01 — THE TOKEN

Built with a clear structure.

GAM is designed as a BNB Smart Chain token with a transparent fixed issuance and no additional minting. Its trading taxes are part of the token mechanism, not a promise of return.

Fixed issuance

100 trillion GAM at deployment, with no future minting in the planned core token contract.

Community allocation

35% initially designated for community rewards, with buy-side GAM fees directed to the community rewards wallet.

True token burns

A 2% sell-side fee is designed to permanently destroy GAM, reducing its on-chain total supply.

04 — GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Can I buy GAM right now?+

No. This is a prelaunch development preview. The site permits wallet connection and illustrative BNB-to-USD conversion, but all transactions are disabled until the sale contract, launch terms and required compliance steps are in place.

How does the USD calculator work?+

The page requests a public BNB/USD market rate and multiplies it by your entered BNB amount. This is indicative, may be delayed, and is not a GAM sale price or a guaranteed exchange rate. The final GAM amount will be quoted by the sale smart contract once deployed.

Does a wallet connection transfer my funds?+

No. Connecting only allows the site to request your public account address and network. Any future purchase will require a separate wallet transaction confirmation, and the presale contract must be verified first. Never share a seed phrase.

How does the 2% trading tax work?+

In the planned GAM token, qualifying trades through registered V2 liquidity pairs incur 2% on buys to rewards, or 2% on sells as a true burn. Other transaction routes may not be classified; fees can also apply to liquidity interactions. The presale contract itself is separate from DEX trading.

Will casino games or Angel Vault be available at launch?+

No gaming launch is being offered or promised. Any future casino or prize-based feature would depend on licensing, approved game mechanics and separate terms. A token purchase does not currently grant access to games or enhanced returns.
